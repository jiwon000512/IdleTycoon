WOMBAT-MADE BURROW WORKSHOP. Built by a wombat from materials found underground: round river stones, packed earth and clay, tree roots, raw logs, a little moss. Earthy palette matching the burrow walls in reference image 2 (warm browns, terracotta, beige-gray stone, a few moss green accents). Oven level 1: a packed-earth clay oven with a few round pebbles pressed into it and a short clay chimney. Oven level 2: a bigger oven of stacked round river stones with clay mortar, a thick curved tree root arching over the mouth, a stone chimney. Shelf: a frame of sturdy twisted root-wood branches holding two thick flat wooden slab tiers. Counter: a thick log slab counter top with visible tree rings on its ends, resting on a base of stacked flat stones, a bit of moss on the stones.
