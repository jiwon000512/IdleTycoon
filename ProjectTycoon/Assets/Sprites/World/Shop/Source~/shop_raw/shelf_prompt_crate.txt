Pixel-art BREAD DISPLAY SHELF for a cozy bakery inside a wombat's underground burrow. Draw ONE object only, centered on a pure white background.

CAMERA: exactly the camera of reference image 1 (a gray blockout of a table and a box). It is a straight-on orthographic view from the front and above:
- we see only TOP faces and FRONT faces, never the left or right side faces, never any underside;
- parallel projection: no perspective, no vanishing point; every vertical edge is perfectly vertical; the back edge of every board is exactly as long as its front edge (rectangles, never trapezoids);
- legs are straight and vertical; the two front legs stand at the front corners and reach the ground; the two back legs are behind them, slightly inward, and end higher up than the front legs because they are farther back, exactly like the blockout table.

STYLE: match reference image 2, a screenshot of the game with the chosen props (a packed-earth clay oven with pebbles, a split-log counter on round river stones, a warm burrow palette, chunky square pixels, cute and soft). Flat colors, 6 to 8 colors, one darker tone for shadow, one small white highlight stroke on the top-left, a 1-pixel dark brown outline (#301818) around the object. No anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering.

GAMEPLAY: it must have a clear, EMPTY, flat display surface where one bread will be placed by the game (draw no bread and no food). Keep the bounding box about 55 wide by 45 tall.

Do NOT draw: text, letters, numbers, price tags, floor, ground shadow, border, background, characters, bread or food.
