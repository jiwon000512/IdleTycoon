Match them exactly: same chunky pixel size (one art pixel = 2 screen pixels at this zoom), same soft muted earthy palette, dark brown outlines (#342020) on objects and characters, same orthographic three-quarter top-down camera (parallel projection, vertical edges stay vertical, no vanishing point), same dug room with the striped earth back wall at the top and a rounded entrance arch in it, same round chubby wombat at the same size as in image 1.
SCENE: the room floor is packed earth like image 1. A WATER CHANNEL dug into the floor runs in a squared S-shape made of straight horizontal and vertical runs joined by right-angle corners: it starts at the left side under the back wall, goes right, turns down, goes left, turns down again, goes right and ends in a round dark DRAIN HOLE in the floor at the lower right where the water disappears. The channel is about as wide as one and a half wombats everywhere and keeps the same width on every run and corner. Because of the top-down three-quarter view, along every horizontal run the FAR (upper) bank shows a short vertical earth face going down to the water and the NEAR (lower) bank shows only a thin lip; vertical runs show both side lips. Small shadow shapes of fish swim in the water. Six empty round wooden stakes stand on the banks next to the channel (no fishing rods yet). The wombat stands on the earth beside the channel. It is deep underground: no sky, no sun, no grass field; warm lantern light.

CHANNEL STYLE:
[a] EARTH DITCH: clear calm teal water; the banks are the same packed earth as the floor, the far banks show a short earth face with the same horizontal strata and thin roots as the back wall, the lips are edged with a few small pebbles; soft white ripple lines on the water.
[b] STONE-LINED CANAL: calm blue-teal water; every bank is lined with rounded grey-brown cobblestones (one row on the lips, two rows down the far faces), neat and built; tiny light glints on the water.
[c] MOSSY ROOT STREAM: darker deep green-teal water with a gentle current; the banks are earth softened by patches of dark green moss and little ferns, thick tree roots hang down the far faces into the water, a few flat stepping stones sit at the edges.
